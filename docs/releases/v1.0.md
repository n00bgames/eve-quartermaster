# EQM v1.0 — The Bigger Slice of PI Release!

**EVE is Excel in a flight suit. Let's give the spreadsheet department a coffee break.**

EVE Quartermaster is stepping out of beta with a bigger helping of Planetary Industry, better tools for keeping your pilots organized, and a little less "hang on, let me do the math."

Feed the factory planet P2. Tell EQM what you've got. See the P3, the P4, what runs out first, and how long the whole operation should take. That's the idea behind **The Bigger Slice of PI Release!**

## In-system distances and warp reference times

Navigation now includes **System Distances & Warp Time** at the bottom: search a solar system, pick an origin and destination, compare AU distances, and enter warp speed plus align time. SDE and ESI provide static-object positions and known structures accessible to your characters. The result is explicitly a rough reference and excludes acceleration and deceleration. [Coverage and setup](https://github.com/n00bgames/eve-quartermaster/blob/main/docs/system-distances.md).

## What's new since v0.1.23.1-beta?

### A proper helping of PI

- **Production Calculator:** choose a final product and the tier you actually feed into your factories. P2 → P3 → P4 chains are supported, including lower-tier ingredients a recipe needs directly. Shared ingredients are counted once, with complete batches, limiting materials, and leftovers shown.
- **Time you can plan around:** set factory counts for each intermediate and the final product. See overlapping production, startup waits, and separate P3/P4 runtimes—with a breakdown for every product running within each tier. The old sequential estimate remains available for comparison.
- **Shortages AND surpluses:** supply reports now put the missing materials and bright-green excess production where you can see them. Colony stock and selected corporate hangar stock have separate columns.
- **Corporate inventory monitoring:** select a station or Upwell corporate hangar division, including stock inside nested containers. Your selected source is remembered in that browser.
- **Less typing:** use **Use selected hangar stock** to fill the calculator, or the little **I** beside an ingredient to import just that quantity while keeping your other entries.
- **Refresh at the source:** a **Sync this corporation's assets** button sits beside the inventory selector. ESI refreshes corporation assets as a whole; it does not offer a location-only sync. Re-import calculator quantities after refreshing. ESI caching still applies.

### Plan the operation before hauling the ingredients

The new **PI Operation Planner** compares production goals, weekly quotas, profits, and buying versus making inputs. It accounts for pilot skills and colony slots, candidate planets, facilities, customs, taxes, setup costs, hauling, and visits.

Market estimates use ESI orders at the selected supported trade hub, including available order depth. Save private scenarios, reopen calculation runs, replay saved prices, and export build/shopping CSVs. The recipe workbench adds inventory-aware dependency graphs and shopping requirements; system scouting helps find suitable planet types; the native template workbench generates and previews starting layouts.

These are planning estimates. Prices do not guarantee fills, scouting does not measure resource density, and generated templates still need placement, routing, and capacity checks in the EVE client.

**More of the number-crunching has been rustified:** native Rust now handles PI allocation, supply calculations, and production-chain math/timing, with Python retaining the application, authorization, and ESI responsibilities.

### Pilots, exports, and clocks

- **PI character groups:** **My Characters Only** is the default. **My Corp Pilots** is available to EQM directors and higher only for corporations where one of their own linked characters has verified corporate roles. An unprivileged alt in another corporation does not open that corporation's pilots. Admins and hosts retain **All Characters**. The selection controls colony totals, supply reports, and exports, with access enforced by the server.
- **Skills exports:** **Export All CSV** and **Export All JSON** cover profiles visible to your account. CSV includes distinct skill and queue rows, queue timing and SP fields, and sync metadata; JSON retains the full queue and totals.
- **EVE and local clocks** now live in the top bar. Fleet time and your time, together.
- Clicking the authenticated **EQM logo returns to Overview**.
- Application versions are prepared for **1.0.0**, published under tag **v1.0**, with the beta/prerelease label retired. Operator documentation now covers deployment and the PI tools.

## More than a PI calculator

EQM is a **self-hosted EVE Online operations suite** for pilots, corporations, and alliances. The rest of the toolbox comes along for the ride:

| Area | What's in the box |
| --- | --- |
| Pilots & readiness | Character dossiers, corporation rosters, skills and queues, editable skill plans and prerequisites, jump clones and implants, ESI/SSO linking, sync visibility, and standings/contact tools. |
| Inventory & industry | Assets, ownership and locations, BPO/BPC and ME/TE tracking, SDE recipes, manufacturing records, research projects, contracts, and private notes/resupply lists. |
| Mining & income | Persistent mining history, residue-aware analysis, mining-operation settlements and payouts, private bounty analytics, and historical wallet/asset reporting. |
| Markets & trade | Market appraisal, Corporate Exchange listings and auctions, and the manual-first HyperNet Tracker with offer economics and results tracking. |
| Navigation & intel | Route planning, gatechecks, operational maps, jump-capable ship routing and fuel estimates, last-hour traffic, industrial/PvP intel, and Local Threat analysis. |
| Fleets & community | Calendars, RSVPs and fleet registration, doctrines, SRP intake/review/analytics, killboards, battle reports with shareable snapshots, and recruiting pages and review workflows. |
| Administration | Roles, section permissions, invitations, privacy controls, notifications, private messages, audit history, and historical analytics/exports. |

The suite uses React, Python/FastAPI, Rust calculation engines, PostgreSQL, and Redis through Docker Compose. Source is available under **AGPL-3.0-or-later**. An optional Android WebView wrapper can also be built for sideloading.

### A word about the Fitting Manager / Simulator

**Non-Authoritative. For Informational Use Only. Still in development. Not PYFA-complete.**

Saved fittings, imports, editable drafts, and simulation tools are available, but full feature and calculation parity with PYFA is not claimed. Check consequential fitting decisions against PYFA and the EVE client. Calling the suite 1.0 does not make this module finished.

## Requirements & installation

**Difficulty: approachable if you're comfortable following a terminal guide; moderate if Docker and EVE SSO are new to you.** The semi-automated scripts handle much of the setup, but this is a self-hosted application rather than a hosted signup service.

- **OS:** 64-bit Windows 11 with WSL2, or a current 64-bit Linux distribution.
- **Runtime:** Docker Desktop, or Docker Engine with Docker Compose v2; Git and reliable internet access.
- **Recommended capacity:** 4 CPU cores, 8 GB RAM available to Docker / 16 GB host RAM, and at least 20 GB free disk space to start. Retained data needs room to grow.
- **EVE setup:** a CCP Developer application for ESI/SSO, plus an imported EVE SDE for the full item, recipe, and skill-data features.

Run **`install-eqm.bat`** on Windows or **`./install-eqm.sh`** on Linux. The helpers check prerequisites, can offer supported prerequisite installation, prepare local configuration and secrets, and build/start the containers. You'll still create the CCP application, set its callback/configuration, fetch/import the SDE, and link your characters. Public hosting also needs HTTPS and reverse-proxy configuration.

**Allow roughly 15–30 minutes with Docker already working, or 30–60+ minutes for a fresh setup.** These are planning estimates: hardware, downloads, restarts, SDE import, initial syncs, and public-server configuration can take longer.

**[Start with the installation guide](https://github.com/n00bgames/eve-quartermaster#install)** · [SSO setup](https://github.com/n00bgames/eve-quartermaster#eve-sso-configuration) · [SDE import](https://github.com/n00bgames/eve-quartermaster#sde-import)

**Upgrading?** Back up your database, then use the documented `update-eqm.bat` / `update-eqm.sh` workflow. This update includes migration `0079_pi_planning`; rebuild the backend and frontend together. The normal Docker build packages Rust and backend startup applies migrations. See the [changelog](https://github.com/n00bgames/eve-quartermaster/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) and [PI guide](https://github.com/n00bgames/eve-quartermaster/blob/main/docs/pi-supply-and-production.md).
