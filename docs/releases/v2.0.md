## 📡 Local Scan and D-Scan: paste first, squint less

**Local Scan** turns a pasted local list into alliance and corporation groups, complete with recognizable logos, counts and expandable pilot lists. Open a corporation to see pilot portraits; click a portrait to head to zKillboard.

Save corporations and alliances you want marked **red**, add or remove them quickly, and keep those preferences between sessions. Changes immediately recolor the open signed-in results; shared snapshots keep the markings saved when they were generated.

Set the system manually, see the pilot total at the top, and use **Set Destination** to choose which of your linked characters get the waypoint.

![Local Scan: system totals, organization groups and pilot portraits](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/n00bgames/eve-quartermaster/main/static/ss/v2.0/local-scan-summary-portraits.jpg)

**D-Scan** groups pasted results into hulls, ship classes, drones/fighters, deployables and structures, with item icons, counts and a base hull-mass estimate. Ships and classes sort from Titans downward. Known combat recons can be added manually, and you can attach a Local Scan for the pilot picture alongside the ship picture. D-scan ship names do not magically identify their owners.

Both scans show their system and total pilots/ships prominently. Big results expand with the page, so you can stop scrolling a tiny report inside another scrolling page.

![D-Scan: ship totals, hulls, classes and deployables](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/n00bgames/eve-quartermaster/main/static/ss/v2.0/dscan-system-total.jpg)

## 🕵️ Intel Reports: remember the visitor before the killmail

Build dossiers on outside pilots, corporations and alliances using public identity and retained combat evidence. Review ships, systems, geography, recorded UTC combat activity, recurring co-attackers, and supporting evidence for things like smartbomb activity. Date filters and collection status help show what the report actually covers.

Spotted someone without a fight? Enter the **pilot, system and ship** to add a sighting to their timeline. Record it now or enter an older UTC time; EQM keeps the observation time separate from when it was posted.

Only know the system? That works too: **Unknown Ship Type**, with a silver-bordered question-mark icon. Sightings contribute **0 kills / 0 losses**. Seeing somebody in local does not mean we get to invent a killmail.

Corporation and alliance logos, names and tickers sit beside the pilot portrait, linking to their respective zKillboard pages. Analyst notes can be edited or deleted by their author, with Admin/Host moderation and protection against overwriting someone else’s newer edit.

## ⚔️ “Remember when they dropped dreads on us?”

You remember the alliance, a Revelation, and roughly how badly outnumbered you were. The date? Absolutely no fucking idea.

**Saved Battle Report search** now lets you combine:

- **My Side / Hostile Side**.
- Pilot, corporation and alliance names.
- Ship class, deployable or structure class, then a specific hull/object.
- Combatant ranges from **Solo** through **1,001+**.
- System, generation-date range, and **AND / OR** detail matching.

Search covers all accessible saved reports, not just the first page. Results show the battle time, systems, side totals and hull summaries, so you can find the fight and get back to arguing about what actually happened.

Battle Reports also gain scoped saved-report management, deletion review, privacy/retention options, and detailed branded PDFs with team metrics, composition, pilots and the timeline. Deletion reviews use the same filters as the search.

![Find an older capital engagement by side, organizations, hulls, numbers and system](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/n00bgames/eve-quartermaster/main/static/ss/v2.0/battle-search-results.jpg)

## 🏭 Industry, Inventory and Ship Reports: three different questions

### Industry Report — what can we build, and what’s already running?

Active jobs come first, followed by **BPOs/BPCs and reaction formulas**, overall stock totals, ownership breakdowns and PI infrastructure. Minerals and PI materials are totalled, and resolved NPC station/Upwell facility names make the locations useful.

Choose particular station cans or hangars to include finished stock such as ammunition, drones or ships. Personal fitted modules and random cargo do not get swept into public industry stock.

Public Industry Reports hide character identities behind labels such as **Character Owned**. Public links are opt-in, expiring and deletable. Optional **dynamic** links rebuild from the latest synchronized database stock when opened, subject to current permissions; they do not run a fresh ESI sync on every visit.

![Industry Report: active jobs first, named facilities and anonymous ownership](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/n00bgames/eve-quartermaster/main/static/ss/v2.0/industry-jobs-first.jpg)

### Inventory Report — where did I leave all my other shit?

Separate, creator-only reports cover the broader personal inventory: ships, modules, fitted items and container stock. **Totals first, itemized owner/location detail underneath**, with sortable tables and CSV/PDF exports. No public Inventory Report links.

### Ship Reports — how many hulls do we actually have?

Get total hull counts, totals by type and class, and private character/corporation/location breakdowns. Packaged stacks count properly. Capsules are excluded, including from old reports.

Filter individual ship classes or hit **Subcaps** to leave shuttles and XL/XXL hulls out. Ship Analytics records synchronized hull totals, distinct types and per-hull history as new observations arrive. **Fleet Commander and above** can see capital Analytics; lower roles get noncapital results, with the restriction enforced on the server and exports.

![Ship Report class filters and the Subcaps preset](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/n00bgames/eve-quartermaster/main/static/ss/v2.0/ship-class-exclusions.jpg)

Industry and Inventory tables now sort numbers, dates and names across the full result before pagination. Blueprint/material stock in validated corporate offices and deliveries is included correctly. CSV/PDF exports carry EQM branding and version attribution.

## 🛡️ Give people the job, not the keys to every hangar

Accounts can hold **multiple roles**, with permissions combined.

- **Quartermaster:** view eligible authenticated characters’ and linked corporations’ blueprint holdings, running industry jobs and planetary infrastructure. A scoped **Sync All** refreshes the relevant data. Corporation feeds still require the actual ESI/in-game access to read them.
- **Intel Analyst:** access Intel Reports and Navigation tools without automatically enrolling their own characters into private inventory/skill collection.
- **Fleet Commander:** an assignable role below Director, with fleet Ship Reports and capital hull Analytics access.
- **Navigation and Market:** available by default to every role.

Consent, refresh scopes and collection follow the character owner’s combined permissions. Using utility tools should not quietly turn into “surprise, we’re tracking your entire account.” Existing privacy opt-outs, ownership checks and visibility boundaries still apply.

**Fleet Assignments** also joins the planning toolbox: composition presets, saved doctrine/fitting snapshots, pasted fleet rosters, multi-pilot drag-and-drop assignment, and keyboard/button alternatives. Published member links require sign-in and show that account’s own verified characters’ assignments.

## 🎨 Your navbar. Your panels. Your questionable color choices.

Choose a theme, set custom colors, upload a personal banner, and drag panels into an order that suits your workflow. Rename and reorder navigation sections, and move modules between them. Preferences persist across sessions, with keyboard movement controls and separate resets.

Want a **Quicklaunch** menu at the top? Create a section, pick tools from a dropdown spanning your accessible navigation, and keep the original links too. Arrange or remove shortcut copies independently.

Admins can upload the site banner and login-page background. Please use this power responsibly. Or at least entertainingly.

## 🔐 SSO, sharing and keeping account names out of intel

**Log in with EVE SSO**, or accept an invitation and link a character with identity-only consent. Invited roles remain authoritative, and ownership, revoked-link and invitation checks apply. Password login remains available, with single-use recovery links and administrator reset controls; email recovery needs the operator’s SMTP configuration.

Set your **main character** in Profile or after linking a character. Named scan/Battle attribution uses the main character rather than the account name, including existing named reports, with Anonymous as the fallback.

Local Scan, D-Scan and Battle Reports support **anonymous/private** generation, branded PDFs, and public retention choices from **30 minutes to permanent**. Owners, directors and administrators manage deletion within their permitted scope. Private reports require authentication and do not generate public links.

![EVE SSO invitation acceptance with assigned roles](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/n00bgames/eve-quartermaster/main/static/ss/v2.0/eve-sso-invite.jpg)

## 💰 Market history, complete exports and less waiting around

Click an item in **Market Appraisal** to expand its latest five regional trading dates: units, average, low and high prices. Switch hubs and compare current hub buy/sell orders beside the completed regional history. Those are different things, and EQM labels them accordingly.

![Expandable regional trade history and current hub orders](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/n00bgames/eve-quartermaster/main/static/ss/v2.0/market-item-history.jpg)

The Asset Ledger can **Load all records** for accessible totals and browsing, or download complete filtered **CSV and Janice lists** without first loading every page. Janice quantities are aggregated and leave owner/location details out.

Under the hood: SQL-filtered asset searches, bounded database pools that expand for bursts, scoped stale-query/abandoned-transaction recovery, and heavy report work moved off the main API event loop. Large reports have less opportunity to hold the login page hostage. The recovery link also has some breathing room now.

**SDE maintenance** is available in Settings: download CCP’s SDE, validate it, optionally import it, and follow worker progress across reloads. Imports use the worker queue, overlapping operations are blocked, and stored/import totals include the nested celestial, Dogma, blueprint and PI details. Because yes, I wanted all the counters.

![SDE maintenance with a completed download receipt](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/n00bgames/eve-quartermaster/main/static/ss/v2.0/sde-maintenance-complete.jpg)

## 🎲 A little more HyperNet bookkeeping

Account-wide and per-character pause controls stop new offers/bids while leaving history and corrections available. Won your own item back and then sold it? Record the sale and fees so the lifecycle result counts acquisition, seeded nodes and HyperCores once. Corrected partial node counts can also release an unresolved offer from its mistaken “fully sold” state.

The screenshot stitching introduced in **v1.0.7** is still there; these are the bits added since that release.

## 🔗 Open that item in the actual game

Item actions can open an **unsent EVE Mail draft** on your chosen linked character, carrying a clickable Show Info link. Existing filters and Market history clicks keep working; use the adjacent arrow, or right-click/Alt+Enter on item labels that already have an action.

Owned **BPOs and BPCs** use their retained item-instance ID when available, with synced **ME, TE and remaining copy runs** in the preview and draft. Generic references fall back to the item type; EVE’s own permissions still determine which instance details the selected pilot can inspect.

This uses the **open in-game windows** permission. It does not request inbox access for this feature, send mail automatically, or transfer ISK. Keep the selected character logged into EVE.

![Owned BPC link with ME, TE, remaining runs and character selection](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/n00bgames/eve-quartermaster/main/static/ss/v2.0/blueprint-client-link.jpg)

## 🛠️ Updating your installation

Back up the database, follow the [README update instructions](https://github.com/n00bgames/eve-quartermaster#readme), and update the backend, worker and frontend together. Since v1.0.7 this includes migrations **0087 through 0100**. SDE maintenance needs the documented writable worker mount; SMTP is optional unless you want email recovery delivery.

The fitting simulator is still a work in progress and **not PYFA-complete**. Keep PYFA and the EVE client handy when a fitting decision involves something expensive.
