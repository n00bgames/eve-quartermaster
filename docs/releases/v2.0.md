# EVE Quartermaster v2.0 — development release draft

**Unreleased draft.** Features and screenshots below are prepared in the 2.0.0 working tree. Public image links become available after the assets are pushed. Update this draft as the release grows; this file does not publish a release.

Local Scan turns a pasted pilot list into a public alliance and corporation breakdown, with recognizable logos, count badges, pilot details and saved red organization highlights. Choose a system, send its destination to selected linked characters, share a timed report, or export a branded PDF. Report owners, corporation directors and administrators get deletion controls within their permitted scope.

ESI authorization and collection follow the token owner's combined permissions, so utility-only accounts do not automatically contribute private assets, skills or industry data.

## Your workspace, your layout

Choose a preset palette or custom colors, upload a personal banner, and arrange EQM around your workflow. Drag panels, rename and reorder navigation sections, and move modules between groups. Preferences follow your account across sessions; keyboard controls and separate reset buttons are included. Admins can also upload the public site banner and login background.

Create a Quicklaunch navigation section and add your frequently used tools through a dropdown covering every section you can access. Keep the original links, move Quicklaunch to the top, and reorder or remove its shortcuts independently. Save once and your layout follows your account.

## Account recovery and complete asset downloads

Forgot your password? Request a single-use recovery link or ask an administrator to create one. Administrators can require a reset and immediately sign out existing sessions. Password changes invalidate old sessions across devices. Email recovery uses configurable SMTP; admin-generated links work without it.

The Assets ledger now downloads complete filtered CSV and Janice lists across every page, without loading all records into the table. Janice downloads aggregate item quantities and leave owner/location information out.

## SDE maintenance without a terminal

Administrators can download CCP’s latest YAML SDE from Settings, validate it, and optionally queue an import. Progress survives page reloads, completed archives include build/checksum receipts, and a shared lock prevents overlapping SDE operations. Existing folder/ZIP and agents-only imports run through the same worker.

### EVE SSO and ship inventory

**Log in with EVE SSO** using a linked character, or accept an invite and link a first character with identity-only consent. Invited roles remain authoritative; transferred characters, revoked links and expired/revoked invites cannot unlock accounts. Password sign-in remains available. Requires migration 0098 and matching API/frontend.

![EVE SSO invitation acceptance](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/n00bgames/eve-quartermaster/main/static/ss/v2.0/eve-sso-invite.jpg)

**Ship Reports** total every accessible synchronized hull, including packaged stack quantities, then show counts per type and private character/corporation/location detail. Sort tables, export CSV/PDF, retain snapshots and manage saved reports. No public ship links are generated.

![Private ship counts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/n00bgames/eve-quartermaster/main/static/ss/v2.0/ship-report-summary.jpg)

Expensive report work now runs outside the main API loop with bounded concurrency. Login recovery has clearer spacing. [Behavior, deployment and verification limits](../sso-and-ship-reports.md). These synthetic development previews do not claim a production deployment or completed live CCP sign-in.

### Fleet visibility and ship history

**Fleet Commander** is an assignable role below Director with access to fleet Ship Reports and capital hull Analytics. Lower roles receive noncapital ship totals, type lists, history and exports. Choose individual ship-class exclusions or the **Subcaps** preset when generating reports; capsules disappear from new and existing Ship Reports. Track synchronized total hulls, distinct types and individual hull-type history with privacy checks and the existing retention policy. New history starts with asset-sync/manual observations.

### Editable analyst notes and market history

**Intel Report notes** now support author edits/deletion and Admin/Host moderation, with stale-change protection and audit metadata. Intel Analyst utility/custom-base roles can enter the workspace with section permission.

**Click an item in Market Appraisal** to expand its latest five regional trading dates, units traded, average/low/high prices and current hub orders. Switch hubs in the summary, refresh through ESI's cache, and scroll expanded results with the page. Regional completed sales and station orders are labelled separately.

### Intel sightings without an engagement

Capture a pilot's ship and solar system beside the name search, then open the character report to record the sighting. Use the report-run time or enter an older UTC time; retain when it was posted separately. The **Timeline** shows analyst-sourced sightings with **0 kills / 0 losses**, while ships, systems and daily summaries include separate sighting counts. Combat evidence and statistics remain unchanged. Requires migration 0099 and matching frontend/API.
