# Quartermaster role

Assign the built-in **Quartermaster** role in User Administration, or invite a user with that role. Open **Inventory & Industry → Quartermaster**, or `#quartermaster`. Hosts and administrators also have the workspace. Other roles require an explicit section grant; existing officers and directors do not automatically gain this organization-wide oversight. Section permissions can disable the workspace.

This is a view-only role, ranked alongside View Only rather than Officer. It does not grant general access to other pilots' wallets, skills, mail, contracts, other assets, user administration or ESI credentials. The authenticated API rejects POST, PATCH, PUT and DELETE requests from Quartermasters except the dedicated **Sync All** endpoint and scoped Navigation analysis/lookups, Market appraisal, and selected-character waypoint operations. Direct general ESI sync, queue, settings, inventory edits, and administrative calls remain blocked. Login and logout remain available. Custom roles based on Quartermaster inherit the restriction; granting section visibility does not remove it. An additional role can grant its own actions through the combined account permissions.

The workspace includes:

- BPO/BPC items and counts, owner, resolved station/structure, nested container/hangar, ME/TE, licensed runs and sync time. Item counts and BPC runs are separate. Active research location shadows are deduplicated against inventory. Unresolved locations stay explicitly labelled.
- Active, paused and ready-to-deliver manufacturing, research, copying, invention and reaction jobs, with installer, corporation where applicable, facility, runs, expected end and sync timestamp. A character's own job remains visible if its canonical record was refreshed through a corporation feed.
- Planetary colonies for all eligible characters: planets, systems, command centers, pins, extractor/factory status, routing, contents, storage, checkpoint freshness and estimated production. PI is character-owned; there is no separate corporation-owned PI inventory. Cached links and routes are available in the response.

Character eligibility excludes utility-only owner accounts, even with legacy broad tokens, and requires an active owner account, a non-revoked token belonging to that current owner, the relevant dataset scope, and no character sync opt-out. Private asset visibility does not suppress these specifically authorized oversight datasets. Stale records from revoked, transferred, unlinked or deleted owners are excluded. Ordinary character-detail and asset permissions are unchanged.

Corporate blueprint and job visibility is checked separately. For each current linked corporation, EQM uses an eligible linked character's existing dataset scopes, verifies current affiliation, and probes the actual corporate ESI read endpoint. ESI therefore enforces the required in-game roles; mere corporation membership or an OAuth scope string is insufficient. Successful verification is cached for up to 60 seconds, while local revocation/ownership/opt-out changes are checked on every view. An outage or denied feed is excluded with a warning, and an alternate eligible token may authorize it. All authorized linked corporations are included, independently of analytics inclusion settings. The read probe refreshes SSO credentials as necessary but does not import inventory, jobs, or colonies.

Records are cached ESI snapshots. **Sync All** starts a bounded background refresh for blueprint inventories and supporting asset locations, industry jobs, and planetary colonies only. It selects eligible tokens per dataset, deduplicates character/corporation tasks, retries alternate authorizations, rechecks role/ownership/revocation/opt-out at execution, and continues past individual failures. Corporate reads are enforced by ESI at execution. Successful complete blueprint reads remove stale ESI blueprint entries while preserving manual records; successful job reads retire missing active jobs as no longer reported within that exact character/corporation feed. Read-only eligibility never authorizes unrelated wallet, skill, mail, contract, mining or contact syncs. Progress reconnects on page reload in the same backend process; process restarts discard the progress handle, but completed imports and ESI job receipts remain stored. A new Sync All can refresh the records again. Refresh view only rereads records; ESI cache expiry still governs source freshness. Missing records do not establish that a pilot has no blueprints, jobs or colonies. PI projections retain the existing estimate and stale-checkpoint notices.

The Quartermaster role itself introduced no database migration. The combined 2.0 update requires migrations 0090/0091 for Local Scan and multiple roles; see [Local Scan and role privacy](local-scan.md). Rebuild backend and frontend together. Validation uses synthetic databases and a local browser harness; no live accounts are assigned and no production records are changed during development.
